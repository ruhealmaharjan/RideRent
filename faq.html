A LITTLE CLARITY

Frequently asked questions.

Browse the fleet, choose dates, log in, and confirm the booking summary. Your booking starts as Pending until the rental desk confirms it.

Bring a valid driving licence suitable for your chosen vehicle and government-issued identification. The rental desk verifies originals at pickup.

For this academic demonstration, the assumed minimum age is 21. A real rental operator must set eligibility according to its policy and applicable rules.

Payment is handled by the rental desk. This demonstration records Cash, Card, Bank Transfer, or Online payments; it does not process money or store card details.

Any security deposit must be confirmed with the rental desk before pickup. The online estimate includes only the daily rental rate multiplied by rental days.

You can cancel Pending or Confirmed bookings from My Bookings. Completed, and Cancelled bookings cannot be cancelled. Contact the desk about any refund.

For the demonstration policy, return the vehicle with the same fuel level recorded at pickup. Confirm the actual agreement with the desk.

Contact the rental desk before your return time. An extension depends on availability and must be agreed before continuing the rental.

Stop using the vehicle if it is unsafe and contact the rental desk. Record the incident; responsibility and any charges depend on the signed agreement.

Still have a question? Contact the rental desk
