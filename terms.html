PROJECT POLICIES

Rental terms.

Academic demonstration

RideRent is a BCA semester project. Sample vehicles, accounts, bookings, contact details, and prices are for demonstration. These terms are sample operating policies, not a real rental contract.

Eligibility and licence

The assumed minimum age is 21. A valid licence for the chosen vehicle and identity documents must be presented before pickup.

Rental duration and pricing

Pickup and return dates define the rental period. Return must be after pickup. Each date-to-date day is charged at the vehicle’s daily rate. Return day is the exclusive boundary; a vehicle may be rented again on that date. Quotes do not include an unspecified deposit or additional charges.

Payment and confirmation

Bookings begin as Pending. The rental desk confirms the reservation and records payment separately. No money is collected by this demonstration website.

Cancellation

Customers may cancel Pending and Confirmed reservations. Completed, and Cancelled reservations cannot be cancelled. Refunds, if relevant, require rental desk handling.

Vehicle use

Use the vehicle lawfully, within agreed limits, and only with authorized drivers. Do not transfer a reservation or use a vehicle for prohibited activities.

Fuel and condition

Record vehicle condition and fuel at pickup. Return the vehicle in the agreed condition and with the recorded fuel level.

Damage and late return

Report damage or breakdown promptly. Request extensions before the return date. Any additional charge must be agreed with the rental operator.
