PROJECT POLICIES

Your information, explained.

Academic demonstration

This notice describes this local semester project. Do not enter real sensitive information in publicly shared demonstration deployments.

Information collected

The application stores your name, email, phone, address, driving licence number, password hash, booking records, contact messages, and submitted reviews. Administrators record payment references and statuses; card numbers are not collected.

How information is used

Account details support authentication and rental administration. Booking records support availability checks and your rental history. Contact messages allow the desk to respond to inquiries. Review text and the reviewer’s name are visible with vehicle reviews.

Sessions and security

The server uses an HTTP-only session cookie. Remember me optionally stores a persistent cookie for up to 30 days with a hashed server-side token. Passwords are hashed, and protected APIs check account permissions.

Booking records and retention

Cancelled rentals remain in the database for history. This academic project has no automatic account-deletion or retention schedule. A real operator must establish an appropriate retention policy.

Password recovery

Password recovery generates an expiring one-time token and sends a reset link through the configured PHP mail service. Raw passwords are never sent by email.

Contact and access

Use the contact form to ask about your records. Demonstration contact details on the Contact page must be replaced before real deployment.
